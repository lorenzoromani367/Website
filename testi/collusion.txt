These photographs focus on where different spaces and surfaces collide. Contrasting lines, rough materials, and ordinary shapes share the same frame, even when they seem to have nothing in common.

The framing flattens depth, forcing what is close and what lies far behind onto the same surface, making distant things touch.

Cut off from their surroundings, these clashes stop feeling like mistakes or random intrusions. Even the coarsest or most awkward parts find a balance: elements never meant to meet come together right where they touch
